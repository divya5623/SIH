## 🎯 The Problem

For many citizens, reporting local problems and accessing government services remains difficult due to language barriers, limited digital literacy, and complicated administrative processes. Traditional complaint systems can make it challenging for citizens to communicate their concerns, track progress, and receive timely updates.

## 💡 Our Solution

**Awaaz Sarpanch** is a voice-first, AI-powered platform designed to make local governance more accessible, transparent, and citizen-friendly. It aims to simplify communication between citizens and local authorities by making it easier to report community issues and access relevant information through an intuitive digital interface.

## 🌟 Key Objectives

* 🎙️ **Voice-First Accessibility:** Make interaction easier for users who find text-based systems difficult.
* 🌐 **Inclusive Communication:** Reduce barriers to accessing local governance services.
* 🏘️ **Community Problem Reporting:** Help citizens communicate local issues through a digital platform.
* 📊 **Transparency:** Support better visibility into reported issues and their progress.
* 🤖 **AI Integration:** Use AI to simplify citizen interactions and improve the overall user experience.
